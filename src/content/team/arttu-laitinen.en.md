Arttu Laitinen is a father of two, a technology and sales professional (M.Sc. Eng., University of Turku), and an active reserve officer. Arttu champions a market-driven, energy self-sufficient Finland that invests in education, putting resources into children, young people, and the future rather than pensioners. Arttu serves as a deputy member of the Turku City Council, deputy chair of the Green council group in Turku, and a member of the Turku Children's and Youth Services Committee.

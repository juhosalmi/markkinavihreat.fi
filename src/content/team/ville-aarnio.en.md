Ville Aarnio is a philosopher, investor, and forest owner who chairs Talousvihreät (the Economy Greens) and is also active in Maaseutu- ja erävihreät (the Rural and Outdoor Greens) and serves as a Green party council member. Academically, he specializes in the foundations of economic and social theory, such as game theory and decision theory. In politics, Aarnio seeks to reconcile different interests, such as economic value creation and nature conservation. His particular areas of expertise are capital taxation and forestry.

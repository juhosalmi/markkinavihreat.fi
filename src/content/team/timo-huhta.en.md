Timo Huhta is an M.Sc. in Energy Engineering, a construction management consultant, a doctoral researcher, and a deputy councillor from Vantaa. Huhta's goal is to build a financially sustainable and just welfare state, which includes sustainable growth and urban development, successful businesses, vibrant and pleasant neighborhoods, and investment in the wellbeing of children and young people. His political themes especially include land and housing policy, urban development, and energy and climate policy.

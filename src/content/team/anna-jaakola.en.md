Anna Jaakola is a specialist in gynecology and obstetrics, an entrepreneur, and the mother of a young son, from Pyhtää. She values politics that puts the wellbeing of future generations ahead of short-sighted self-interest, and that sees humanity, empathy, and civilization as strengths of society. Anna believes a thriving economy is built on a stable society, education, research, and respect for nature's carrying capacity. In politics, she is motivated especially by the wellbeing of children and young people, nature conservation, humanity and social justice, science-based decision-making, and a forward-looking, responsible market economy.

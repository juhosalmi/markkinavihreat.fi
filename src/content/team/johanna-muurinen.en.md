Johanna Muurinen is a researcher, docent, and agronomist from Sipoo, and a novice municipal politician who entered politics to help ensure that decisions are based on research evidence. On the Sipoo Municipal Council, she is 1st deputy councillor, a member of the Building and Environment Committee, a deputy member of the Municipal Executive Board, and the Executive Board's representative on the board of the Sipoo Vesi water utility. Johanna is originally from Porvoo and has also lived in the United States, where she saw up close where value-conservative, science-dismissive politics leads. Professionally, Johanna researches humanity's existential questions, and her political goal is to steer decisions so that the world becomes a better place for future generations.

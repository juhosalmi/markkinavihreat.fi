Taru Hallikainen is a humanist and feminist from Oulu, and mother of three teenage sons. She works as a career coach and is a deputy councillor on Oulu's city council. Taru comes from an entrepreneurial family and has herself worked as a sole trader. In politics, her focus has been on democracy, nature, and human rights.

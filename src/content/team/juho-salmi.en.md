Juho is a management researcher, advisor, and speaker who does satire and stand-up about the most absurd phenomena in working life. In his view, Finnish management and politics suffer from the same afflictions: a visionless inability to reform, short-sighted self-interest, and a gap between words and deeds. Market Greens dismantle the corporate subsidies that hold back reform, put an end to environmentally destructive free-riding, and put a price on externalities that ties actions to words. This is how sustainable markets for green growth and new frontiers are created.

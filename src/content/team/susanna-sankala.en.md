Susanna Sankala is a lifelong-learning specialist at the doctoral school of LUT University (M.Soc.Sc.), an education-sector entrepreneur, and a speaker from Turku. Susanna's main campaign themes are education, security, and the future. She is especially active in networks around artificial intelligence, technology, and entrepreneurship. She chairs the Greens' expert working group on education policy and chairs the Turku Region Green Women.

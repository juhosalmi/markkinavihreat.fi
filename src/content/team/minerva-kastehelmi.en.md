Minerva Kastehelmi, from Lahti, is an engineer in industrial management with a Bachelor of Social Services, and is studying for a Master of Science (Technology). She works as a public procurement specialist.

Minerva wants to promote evidence-based, far-sighted decision-making. She cares in particular about a sustainable market economy, employment, well-functioning basic services and security.

In Lahti, Minerva is a member of the city board and the city council. She is also a member of the Päijät-Häme wellbeing services county council and its audit committee.

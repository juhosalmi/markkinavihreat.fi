Jaakko Kyrö is a father from Mäntsälä, a software developer, and a folk musician. Alongside his day job, his time is spent in municipal politics as a deputy member of the Mäntsälä Municipal Council and a member of the Technical Committee, as well as in the cultural sphere promoting Finnish folk music. The Greens became Jaakko's party primarily because of its market-friendliness, its lack of special-interest politicking, and its evidence-based approach to policy.

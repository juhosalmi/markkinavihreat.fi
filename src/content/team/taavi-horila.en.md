Taavi Horila is an integration architect, holds a Master of Social Sciences degree, chairs the Seinäjoki Greens (Seinäjoen Vihreät ry.), and is a member of the Green League's party council. For him, the most important goal of politics is keeping the planet habitable, and Horila wants to do his part toward that. The market economy is the best possible way to allocate resources fairly. With the right boundary conditions and by pricing externalities, the market economy is also a fair way for everyone to solve the climate and nature crisis.

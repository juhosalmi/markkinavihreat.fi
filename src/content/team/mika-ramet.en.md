Mika Rämet is Professor of Paediatrics at the University of Oulu and Professor of Experimental Immunology at Tampere University. Alongside his academic work, he is active in the Greens, promoting evidence-based decision-making.

Mika's aim is to bring science and public engagement together and to use research to promote people's health and well-being, especially that of children and young people.

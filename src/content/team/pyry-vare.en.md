Pyry Väre is the Green League's candidate in the Satakunta electoral district in the 2027 parliamentary election, a city councillor in Ulvila, and vice chair of the Green League's party council. He also chairs Satakunnan Vihreät and Ulvilan Vihreät and is vice chair of Eläinvihreät. Outside politics, you'll usually find him training or competing in various dog sports with his two Bernese mountain dogs.

Pyry has previously worked as a sole trader and trained massage therapist, and in office work in waste management. Today he runs an investment company and works on a project basis in the waste sector as a systems specialist and management consultant.

His campaign themes emphasise entrepreneurship — above all, improving the operating conditions of micro and small businesses — and the opportunities of the circular economy.

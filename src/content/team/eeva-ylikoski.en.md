Eeva Ylikoski is studying to become a primary school teacher at the University of Eastern Finland. She serves on the student union board in the social policy portfolio and as deputy chair of the board of the Green Youth and Students of Savo-Karjala. Eeva also sits on the Joensuu Council for Persons with Disabilities. She values politics that helps and supports those with the least, in particular. Nature organizations are also important to Eeva, and it is where she began her own political career. She is involved with the Greens because nature and animals are especially close to her heart. She also believes a strong state helps those who are weaker to cope and stay included in society.

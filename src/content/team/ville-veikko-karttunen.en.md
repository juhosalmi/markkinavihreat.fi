Ville-Veikko Karttunen is a CFO, investor, and active Green Party member. As a father of two, Ville-Veikko considers it absolutely essential that politics be made with a long-term view, on the terms of generations to come rather than those on their way out. The climate crisis is the interest nature charges us after a long-running payday-loan spiral. Now is the time to pay down the principal, not keep living on debt — otherwise, enforced debt collection will cost every one of us our home.

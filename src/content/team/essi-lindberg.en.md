Essi Lindberg is a Turku-based Parliamentary candidate for the Greens of Varsinais-Suomi, a Turku city councillor, a councillor in the Varha wellbeing services county, and a member of the Greens' party council. Professionally, she leads communications at an economic research institute.

For Essi, the market economy is not an end in itself but an effective tool for building wellbeing, innovation, and a fair, green transition. At the core of her thinking is that social justice cannot be credibly defended without understanding where society's wellbeing and the resources being distributed actually come from. That is why the task of politics is not just deciding how existing wealth is divided, but also building the conditions for fair wealth creation, rising labor productivity, and sustainable growth within the limits of one planet.

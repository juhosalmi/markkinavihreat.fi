Susanna Sielo is an Espoo city councillor, a market-green finance enthusiast, a strategy-specialized economist, and a rational voice in politics. Professionally, she consults companies on questions of technology and artificial intelligence. In Espoo, Susanna serves, among other things, on the Business and Entrepreneurship Steering Group, and promotes the conditions for growth companies and entrepreneurship education for all young people. Raising the level of education, advancing equality, and preventing youth exclusion are important themes for Susanna, and she has also gotten these goals through in the council. As a former National Coalition Party voter, Susanna switched to the Greens when the Coalition no longer matched her vision of evidence-based, reformist, resource-wise politics. Susanna would rather build politics that combines a renewing economy, the recovery of nature, and a free, civilized society. Susanna's heart isn't on the right — it's warm, empathetic, and green.

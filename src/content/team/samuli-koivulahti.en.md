Samuli "Sako" Koivulahti is a sole entrepreneur from Turku, a creative-industry professional, and a father of three. He brings straight talk and the everyday realism of a sole trader — including from the creative sector — into green politics. For Sako, it is clear that the welfare state can only be saved through work and entrepreneurship, but not at the expense of nature or future generations.

In politics, Sako fights for a fair market economy and especially for the standing of sole traders and small business owners. He wants to dismantle the bureaucratic contraptions that hold back entrepreneurship, such as the unjust YEL pension system, and to cut competition-distorting corporate subsidies. The Greens are a natural home for Sako, because the party is able to combine economic realism with intergenerational responsibility, without the entrenched special-interest politicking of the traditional right-wing parties.

Lauri Nevanperä is a lead AI engineer, a Tampere city councillor, a member of the Urban Environment Committee, and a Pirkanmaa regional councillor. His core areas are the economy, technology, transport, and urban planning. Lauri believes the market mechanism is the most efficient known way to allocate resources, but that the market economy is not a moral force. That is why we need both free markets and a strong social safety net. A strong economy, a warm heart.

Jukka Ylikarjula works as a director of finance and administration. He is also known as a keen birdwatcher and nature lover, and for his many positions of trust at regional and municipal level.

“I'm standing because decision-making needs experience, a positive belief in the future and the ability to work together. The values that matter to me are fairness, equality, enterprise, responsibility and respect for nature.”

Mikko Särelä holds a doctorate in communications engineering from Aalto University, is the founder of the "More City" (Lisää kaupunkia) movement, and was previously active in Helsinki municipal politics. Mikko also chaired the Greens' urban policy working group in 2022–2023. Over his career, Mikko has worked at a startup, a university, a large international corporation, and in the NGO sector. Markets are an excellent tool for producing wellbeing, allocating scarce resources, and reducing environmental harm. Emissions trading is the best method yet devised for cutting emissions.

Kalle Euro is a champion of architecture, sustainable urban development, and the market economy, who joined the Greens because he felt the party combines faith in the future, civilization, internationalism, and the defense of human rights. After a long history with the National Coalition Party, what proved decisive was the sense that in the Greens, market-driven economic thinking, modern urban policy, and an open society can go hand in hand.

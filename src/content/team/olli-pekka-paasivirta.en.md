Olli-Pekka Paasivirta is a Master of Science in Economics and Business Administration and a deputy city councillor from Espoo. He has worked as an economist both in the private sector and in the public sector. He believes the market economy is the best tool for solving our era's great problems, and that among Finland's parties, the Greens understand this best of all.

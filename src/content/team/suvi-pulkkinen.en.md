I'm Suvi Pulkkinen, a Helsinki city councillor, vice-chair of our council group and a member of the City Board. I'm standing for Parliament to promote good growth: making work pay, renewing the economy and giving every child a good education. Finland now needs a direction that cannot be just higher taxes or lower spending, but a truly strong and sustainable welfare society, where work and skills build wellbeing and prosperity within the planet's limits.

In my day job I work on business, immigration and skills. As a mother of small children, a bonus mum to teenagers and a class teacher by training, it is clear to me that children must come first when decisions are made. They have no voice of their own in decision-making, but it will fall to them to carry the Finland of the future.

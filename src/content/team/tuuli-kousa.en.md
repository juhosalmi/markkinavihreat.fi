Tuuli Kousa is a Helsinki city councillor, a member of the City Board, a lawyer, and an entrepreneur. Tuuli has extensive experience in corporate leadership roles in communications, responsibility, and public affairs. In politics, she has previously served as chair of the Helsinki City Council, as special adviser to Minister of Development Cooperation and Ownership Steering Pekka Haavisto, and as chair of the Greens' economic policy working group.

The cornerstones of Tuuli's politics are a responsible market economy, equality, education, culture, and biodiversity. On this foundation, a civilized, prosperous, and socially just society can be built — one where everyone has prospects and that encourages people to act, dare, and try.

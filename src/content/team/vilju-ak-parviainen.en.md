Vilju is an upper secondary school student heading into his final year, as well as a law student at the Open University. He serves as economic policy officer on the federal board of the Green Youth and Students, and in the Greens' foreign and security policy expert working group. At the core of Vilju's economic philosophy are a sustainable economy, solid comprehensive security, and investment in education as its foundation. Universal basic income, equality, and benefit/harm-based taxation are the keys to a liberated life.

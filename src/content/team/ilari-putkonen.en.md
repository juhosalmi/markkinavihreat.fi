Ilari Putkonen is an economist, lead IT specialist, and municipal politician from Tampere. In politics, Putkonen is especially interested in promoting a fair market economy, creating the conditions for economic growth, and upholding intergenerational justice. Tolerance, the indivisible dignity of every human being, and safeguarding nature's carrying capacity form the value base for Putkonen's political work.

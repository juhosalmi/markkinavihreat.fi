Sofia Alainen is a student of information technology engineering (M.Sc. Eng. track), deputy chair of the board of the Student Union of the University of Turku, and a member of the federal board of the Green Youth and Students. She comes from a multi-generational family farm in South Ostrobothnia and is the first in her farming family to attend higher education.

At the heart of her politics is the idea of a Finland where your starting point does not determine your future — only a person's own ability, ambition, and dreams do. Supporting market economy is a way to achieve that.

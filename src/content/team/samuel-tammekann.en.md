Samuel Tammekann is the acting executive director of Eurooppalainen Suomi ry (European Finland), a special adviser, and a student of social history. Having previously served as 2nd deputy chair of the National Coalition Youth and, since moving to the Greens, been closely involved in numerous campaigns, Tammekann wants to help build an open society and an eco-social market economy resilient to this century's pressures — one where individual freedom, justice, and ecological carrying capacity go hand in hand. He is particularly interested in the resilience of pluralist democracy in an age of technological disruption and overlapping crises.

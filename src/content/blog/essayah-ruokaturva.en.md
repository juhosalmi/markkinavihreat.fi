locale: en
title: Is Essayah and the rest of the government up to the task on food security?
description: Russia's fertilizer shock revealed that Minister Essayah has gotten her priorities wrong. Food security requires self-sufficiency - renewable energy, recycled fertilizers, and a more plant-based diet.
date: 2026-07-05
author: Antti Van Wonterghem
---

According to [YLE News](https://yle.fi/a/74-20234399), Russia's effective decision to halt fertilizer shipments came out of the blue for Minister Essayah, who is responsible for food security, biodiversity loss, and zoonotic disease risk. The war of aggression in Ukraine has now lasted more than four years, and the government has repeatedly stressed the need to break free of dependence on Russia. Has the minister spent three years on [fairy-tale-forest criteria](https://www.hs.fi/politiikka/art-2000010768735.html), [opposing the EU restoration regulation](https://yle.fi/a/74-20090198), [keeping fur farming on life support](https://yle.fi/a/74-20133116), and [culling permits for various animal species](https://yle.fi/a/74-20199146), only to forget the most important thing — food security?

The energy tax refunds planned to ease farmers' situation would only maintain fossil-fuel dependence and, at best, kick the problem further down the road. Instead, measures should be directed at strengthening farms' self-sufficiency by increasing renewable energy production and the use of recycled fertilizers.

Let's start with energy and fuels. The most sustainable solution for self-sufficiency is to make use of waste and side streams. [Biogas potential](https://www.viite.fi/2022/02/21/rajallista-biokaasua-tulee-kayttaa-viisaasti) alone (10 TWh) would be enough to cover [agriculture's energy needs](https://www.luke.fi/fi/tilastot/maa-ja-puutarhatalouden-energiankulutus/maa-ja-puutarhatalouden-energiankulutus-2023) (7.5 TWh). Biofuels should not rely on [dedicated cultivation or ecologically harmful](https://www.helsinki.fi/fi/ruralia-instituutti/biopolttoaineet-tuotettava-luomumenetelmin-vaatii-naturland-jarjesto) biomass feedstocks. In line with the Central Union of Agricultural Producers and Forest Owners (MTK)'s [praise](https://www.maaseuduntulevaisuus.fi/uutiset/0455165c-a672-58e5-a9c1-ff7aedfcb735) for the Green League's [agricultural policy program](https://www.vihreat.fi/ohjelmat/maatalousohjelma/), food material unfit for human consumption should be used in full for biofuel production.

At the farm level, the waste fractions from biofuel production — such as the [rapeseed meal](https://www.theseus.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/16958/kylmapuristetturypsikotielaintenrehuna.pdf) left over from biodiesel production and the mash left over from ethanol production — can be used as animal feed. This would also improve agriculture's weak feed self-sufficiency. Finland's [self-sufficiency in protein feed](https://varsinais-suomi.mtk.fi/-/herneesta-kikherneeseen-uudet-mahdollisuudet-valkuaiskasvien-viljelyssa) currently stands at 27.2%.

Solar and wind power construction on farms should also be increased through various suitable measures. Heat pumps and the use of waste heat should be supported as part of sustainable farm energy solutions.

According to a broad [international research project](https://www.luke.fi/fi/uutiset/kansainvalinen-tutkimus-kierratyslannoitteet-ovat-tehokkaita-ja-turvallisia-kiertotalouden-edistajia-kaytettavyytta-on-viela-parannettava), recycled fertilizers already on the market, made from nutrient-rich side streams, could safely replace mineral fertilizers that depend on fossil energy. Beyond addressing [today's challenges](https://yle.fi/a/74-20234491), the use of recycled fertilizers would also help with problems that have long plagued agriculture, such as the depletion of organic matter in soil and the buildup of nutrients on livestock farms.

In addition to using biobased fertilizers already on the market, nutrient recycling would be made more efficient by [processing manure locally on livestock farms and creating a market-based manure exchange](https://julkaisut.valtioneuvosto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/9c23a1d1-7d6c-46cd-a814-911e011ac3f5/content). These measures would help nutrients that accumulate on livestock farms flow more efficiently to crop farms, and the new markets would strengthen the vitality of rural areas.

Instead of propping up fossil dependence, the ministry should launch a communications campaign to break down prejudices against recycled fertilizers, support their use, and pay attention to [nutrient recovery projects](https://www.tek.fi/fi/opinnayte24). Recycled fertilizers could [largely cover our phosphorus needs](https://www.luke.fi/fi/uutiset/luke-eturintamassa-edistamassa-kierratyslannoitteiden-tehokasta-ja-turvallista-kayttoa-euroopassa). In addition, as many as 72% of European farmlands do not need phosphorus fertilization at all. The need for nitrogen is greater, so hopefully Russia's decision will give momentum to the [Naantali ammonia project](https://yle.fi/a/74-20227066).

Instead of policy that promotes food security and security of supply, Minister Essayah focuses ideologically on maximizing [meat consumption](https://www.suomenmaa.fi/uutiset/lihansyonti-halutaan-panna-kuriin-ministeri-essayah-tyrmaa-uudet-ravitsemussuositukset/), production, and [exports](https://www.kd.fi/kd-lehti/politiikka/maa-ja-metsatalousministeri-essayah-ruokavienti-kaksinkertaistettava/), even though the [global situation](https://www.hs.fi/suomi/art-2000010269493.html) calls for [the opposite measures](https://animaliamedia.fi/ruuantuotannon-omavaraisuus-kasvaa-kasviproteiinien-elintarvikekayttoa-lisaamalla/).

At the [April budget framework session](https://mmm.fi/-/kehysriihen-linjaukset-vahvistavat-huoltovarmuutta-ja-ruuantuotannon-kestavyytta), the government promised to safeguard the conditions for food production amid a difficult economic situation. In practice, however, instead of concrete measures, the ministry decided to carry out a new bioenergy study and to improve the profitability of dairy farms with a [calcium pilot](https://www.mtk.fi/-/kalsiumpilotti), in which farmers themselves administer first aid to cows suffering from milk fever, instead of calling a veterinarian. This seriously endangers animal welfare, since it is difficult to distinguish milk fever from other, equally life-threatening conditions with similar symptoms.

To top it off, the calcium pilot is estimated to save dairy farmers only a few hundred euros a year in costs, while at the same time rising [fertilizer](https://www.iltalehti.fi/kotimaa/a/7dfe8588-f17d-47f3-bb5f-0df86cdeaafb) and [fuel costs](https://stat.fi/fi/julkaisu/cmfyxga5cn7u907w38s0u2cdq) have increased farms' expenses by several thousand euros. The driving force behind the budget framework session's decisions appears to be identity politics, conservatism, and hostility toward expertise, rather than promoting security of supply and food security. The energy tax refund is, in effect, little more than [a token gesture](https://www.uutisrauma.fi/ajassa/viljelijan-hatahuuto-kylla-nama-ymmartavaiset-puheet-ilman-tekoja-kiukuttavat-yha-enemman-6.331.182008.17cca975b4).

In Finland, more than 60% of consumed protein comes from animal-based products. [Shifting to a more plant-based diet](https://www.luke.fi/fi/tietoa-lukesta/vastuullisuus/vastuullisuusraportti-2024/suomi-voi-lisata-merkittavasti-kasviproteiinien-tuotantoa-2040-mennessa-ja-samalla-turvata-huoltovarmuutta-ja-monipuolistaa-ravitsemusta) would make it possible to meet the population's protein needs from domestic production alone. A [reduction in the farmland area needed for feed](https://www.luke.fi/fi/uutiset/puolet-peltoalastamme-rehuntuotannossa-kauran-viljelyala-kasvussa) would also promote [water protection](https://itameri.fi/itameren-tila/rehevoityminen/ravinnekuormituksen-lahteet/).

Microbiological risks also argue in favor of reducing animal production. [Zoonotic](https://onehealth.fi/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Me_ja_maapallo_OHF.pdf) and [pandemic risk](https://elainoikeusakatemia.fi/elaintuotanto-ajaa-uusien-pandemioiden-syntya/) from animal industry is significant, yet still hard to predict, as shown by the emergence of [bird flu on dairy farms in the United States](https://yle.fi/a/74-20135124) — a disease that has threatened poultry for decades. In Finland, bird flu has appeared not only in wild birds but also on [fur farms](https://yle.fi/a/74-20124880), and their [protective measures](https://yle.fi/a/74-20181802) have been implemented inadequately.

Rather than maintaining the status quo, Finland should take a cue from [Denmark](https://www.suomenmaa.fi/uutiset/tanska-laittaa-radikaalin-veron-maatalouden-paastoille-hintalappu-noin-sata-euroa-lehmalta-2/), which has put a radical tax on agricultural emissions. At the same time, sustainable and viable production should be supported judiciously. **Finland needs new thinking that represents green food security, and a [minister responsible for nature's and animals' wellbeing](https://www.maaseuduntulevaisuus.fi/maatalous/fcd15fce-7731-4551-9e12-2bae4364fd6c).**
